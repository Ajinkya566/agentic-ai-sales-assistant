METRO Sales Operations - Sample SOP

1. Sales Reporting:
Sales data should be reviewed monthly.
The sales team should compare product performance across regions.

2. Low Sales Products:
Products with low sales should be reviewed by the sales manager.
The manager should investigate customer demand, pricing, and stock availability.

3. Regional Performance:
Sales performance should be compared across Pune, Mumbai, and Nagpur.
Regional differences should be investigated before taking business decisions.

4. Inventory Planning:
Inventory decisions should consider sales trends and product demand.
High sales do not automatically mean that stock should be increased without checking inventory.

5. Reporting Guidelines:
Reports should use verified sales data.
If information is unavailable, the assistant should clearly state that it cannot find the answer.


Sales Return Policy

Sales Return Policy

Customers can return a product within 7 days.
The product must be unused and in its original packaging.
Damaged products must be reported within 48 hours.
Refunds are processed after the returned product is approved.
